# Film Bible — "The Bottle That Froze at a Knock" (Supercooled Water)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps. **Voice:** `audio/clean.wav`, 66.37 s, 160 words, 28 lines, all times in `audio/clean.json` / `audio/lines.json` (film time = the voice's clock; t = 0 is the first sample).
**Source:** Araby's own voiceover + his word-timed transcript, prepared 2026-11-26. Lines are written **as heard** (Egyptian dialect kept: الإزازة = the bottle, بص = he looked, يا دوب = he'd only just). Nothing in the read differs from the story he described; the science lands in the same order he tells it: the knock → the white line → below zero but liquid → purity → the name (supercooling) → the spark → the chain reaction → the slush → the twist (freezing itself throws heat out — recalescence, told in words, never named).

**Casting decision (binding):** one cast member, the **hero** — the man in the story — is on screen for the whole film and says every line himself (`who: 'hero'`, storytime staging: he tells it to camera while it happens around him / to him). There is no second person in the story; the **bottle is the second actor** — its state changes are the reactions. Reason: the kit's first rule is an expressive face in frame 0 acting every word; a disembodied narrator (`who: null`) would waste the funniest beats (lines 8–10, 24–25 are pure face).

## Colour script

- **Kitchen, morning (beats 1–2, 5):** warm ivory and wood, hard-ish daylight from the window, warm LED pools on the counter; the bottle's cold blue accent grows as it frosts. Beat 5 adds the faintest warm shimmer off the freezing bottle (the heat it throws out).
- **Freezer inserts:** cold blue-white against the warm kitchen rim through the door; frost sparkle.
- **Inside the bottle (beats 3–4):** deep cold teal, near-black edges, one shaft of refracted daylight; beat 3 is the stillest, clearest water in the film — beat 4 whites it out from a single point, the white front as the place's own light event.
- One film, three places, three looks — with the sound off you always know where you are.

## The opening (prove first, per the kit)

Frame 0 is the hero's **face, big** (300 px+), eyes wide, mid-gasp — the bottle already in his raised hand at the frame's lower edge, the first white line visible in it. Shot 1 plays line 1's knock as the cause (he slams it / the moment replays under his reaction), then the film runs forward. The opening gets its own critic round before the rest of the film is acted (Build step 4).

## Notes for the Build pass

- **Arabic captions risk (check first):** captions are `words` style, burned in by the engine. The host's font loading names Vietnamese/Russian/Thai/Hindi/CJK fonts; **Arabic is not in that list** — the pilot must prove Arabic words render correctly (shaped, RTL, a font that carries them) at frame 0 before any full build. If the engine can't shape Arabic, the fallback is decided with Araby, not silently.
- The knock (line 1), the button mime (line 5) and the thermometer snap (line 27) are the three `snap` hits the automatic sound design will land on — keep their frames clean.
- End: last word ends 66.25; the engine's automatic ending (~0.5 s after the last word/action) puts the film end at ≈ 66.75 s.
