The fonts in this folder draw the burned-in captions of a film in the letters the system caption fonts (Segoe UI Black,
Arial Black) can't be trusted with on every computer: Vietnamese, Russian, Thai and Hindi (lab/stage.js capFace). They are
the same files as the app's Blender kit fonts (templates/blender/assets/fonts, built by the app's scripts/blender-fonts.py):
each is Inter Black (Latin, Latin Extended, Vietnamese, Cyrillic; from @fontsource/inter) merged with one Noto Sans script
font at weight 800, named "CZ Latin" (cz-latin.ttf), "CZ Thai" (cz-thai.ttf, + Noto Sans Thai) and "CZ Devanagari"
(cz-deva.ttf, + Noto Sans Devanagari). tools/export_suite.mjs copies them to templates/cartoon/lib/fonts.
